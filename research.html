Digitizing Touch with an Artificial Multimodal Fingertip

Robotics · Haptics · AI · Prosthetics

Digit 360 is an advanced artificial fingertip integrating visuotactile imaging, pressure, vibration, temperature, and gas sensing. With superhuman performance — resolving 7 μm spatial features and detecting millinewton forces — it enables the next frontier in robotics, prosthetics, and virtual reality.
