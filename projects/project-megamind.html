Megamind

Locally-run offline AI personal assistant.

Role
Creator
Year
2026
Status
Active Experiment
Technologies
Local LLM, Python, Speech AI

The Idea

Megamind was born from a desire to have a JARVIS-inspired personal AI environment that does not rely on cloud servers. In an era where every voice query and personal datum is sent to a remote corporate server, Megamind explores what is possible when intelligence runs entirely on local hardware.

The Architecture

The system executes locally without sending telemetry or data to external servers. It provides voice-activated system automation and context-aware intelligence. By keeping the processing loop entirely on-device, it maintains absolute privacy while minimizing latency for system-level commands.
