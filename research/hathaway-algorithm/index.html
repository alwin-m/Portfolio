The Hathaway Algorithm

A Bayesian and Markov approach to menstrual-cycle forecasting, developed as part of Alwin Madhu’s work on privacy-respecting health technology.

The question

The model explores how a cycle tracker can make useful forecasts from a person’s own history without requiring that sensitive health data be sent to a central service.

Approach

The project combines probabilistic reasoning with an offline-first product direction. It is documented here as research connected to LIORA, with claims kept limited to the model and implementation described in the portfolio.
