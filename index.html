I'm Alwin Madhu — a Computer Engineer from Kerala, India. I'm currently pursuing my Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) at Manipal University Jaipur, following a Diploma in Computer Engineering from a government polytechnic college and a Higher Secondary (Plus Two) in Electronics Science. My path runs through hardware fundamentals, systems programming, and applied AI and I like it that way.

My work spans three fronts: computational drug discovery, mesh-network communication, and personal AI. Genome Sentinel is an AI-powered platform for computational drug discovery, combining molecular docking (AutoDock Vina), 3D molecular visualization, and generative chemistry models to accelerate early-stage compound screening. On the health-tech side, I've built predictive period-tracking systems for women's health, including the Hathaway Algorithm — a Bayesian/Markov-based cycle forecasting model documented in a full research paper. Megamind is a locally-run, JARVIS-inspired personal AI assistant with no cloud dependency and no data leaving the machine.

The project closest to my convictions is Scream — a peer-to-peer mesh messaging platform that runs without the internet, using Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi Direct to relay encrypted messages device-to-device. It's built for the moments infrastructure fails: disaster zones, remote regions, and blackouts, where reliable connectivity can't be assumed. Every message is end-to-end encrypted, with no central server to depend on. I built it on one belief: the ability to communicate shouldn't require permission from anyone in between.

I'm interested in the space where computation meets consequence — AI for drug discovery, resilient offline communication, and privacy-respecting personal computing. Most of what I build starts as something I wanted to exist and couldn't find.
